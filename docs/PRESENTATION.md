# GRANTH-AI — short presentation

**Idea Contributor: BHAVESHBHAI SOLANKI · Founder & Developer: DIVYESH BAMBHANIYA**

GRANTH-AI — An initiative conceptualized from an educational idea shared by Bhaveshbhai Solanki and developed and
engineered by Divyesh Bambhaniya. Our mission is to make verified textbook-based knowledge accessible to
Gujarati-medium students.

1. **Problem** – Students get confident but wrong answers from general AI tools, not tied to their textbook.
2. **Idea** – Answer only from the student's own textbook, in its own words, with chapter and page. Otherwise say
   "not found in this textbook".
3. **How** – Pick the book, ask by typing, speaking or photographing the question. The engine finds the passage,
   copies the answer, checks it word for word, and shows the source page.
4. **Also** – Exam-style answers by marks, chapter revision, practice questions, textbook dictionary, offline
   reading.
5. **Cost** – No paid service. No AI API call per question. Runs on one small server.
6. **Proof so far** – 46 of 48 benchmark questions correct on demo books; never answered when it should have
   refused; 121 automated checks pass.
7. **Not yet** – Real GSEB textbooks (permission needed), real-phone testing, handwriting.
8. **Next** – Written permission from the textbook board; pilot with one standard and one subject.
